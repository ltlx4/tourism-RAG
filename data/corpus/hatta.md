# Hatta Mountain Escape

Hatta is an inland mountain destination within the Emirate of Dubai and provides a
contrast to the city. Visitors go for mountain scenery, heritage, hiking, cycling and
seasonal outdoor activities. Hatta Dam is associated with water-based activities such as
kayaking when conditions and operators permit.

The area is not served like central Dubai by the Metro. A planned transfer, tour or car
is normally more practical, and travel time makes Hatta a full-day excursion rather than
a short addition to a city itinerary. Carry water, sun protection and footwear suitable
for the selected trail or activity.

Outdoor facilities can be seasonal and weather-dependent. Trail difficulty, age rules,
equipment, transport and opening arrangements should be verified through official Hatta
and operator pages. Travelers should not begin a mountain route based only on a general
tourism summary.
