# First-Time Dubai Itinerary Design

A practical first-time three-day plan groups Dubai by geography. One day can focus on Old
Dubai: Al Fahidi, the Creek, an abra and Deira's souks. A second can remain around
Downtown Dubai for Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall and nearby activities. A third can cover the
coast through Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, JBR or a selected beach.

A desert excursion can replace part or all of a city day depending on its pickup and
duration. Hatta normally requires its own day. Avoid placing timed bookings in distant
districts back-to-back because traffic, station walks, heat and the size of venues can
consume the apparent gap.

Reserve only the activities where timing matters, then keep flexible meals and walks
around them. Check current transport, opening hours, tickets and weather shortly before
each day. This structure is a planning template, not a statement that every listed venue
will be available.
