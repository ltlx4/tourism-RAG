# Accessible Travel in Dubai

Many modern Dubai hotels, malls, transport facilities and attractions provide accessible
features, but availability differs by venue and route. A wheelchair user should confirm
step-free entrances, lift access, accessible toilets, seating and transfer requirements
directly with each venue before booking.

Metro stations generally include lifts and designated facilities, while accessible taxis
may require advance planning. A route can still contain long indoor walks or exposed
outdoor connections. Ask hotels and operators for specific measurements or assistance
details when general statements such as "wheelchair friendly" are not enough.

Desert, marine and mountain activities require particular scrutiny because vehicles,
boarding points and terrain vary. Travelers with sensory, mobility or medical needs
should communicate requirements in advance and keep contingency time. Official venue and
transport accessibility information takes priority over this summary.
