# Dubai Marina and JBR

Dubai Marina is a waterfront district of high-rise towers, restaurants and pedestrian
promenades around a man-made marina. The Marina Walk is useful for an evening stroll,
while nearby Jumeirah Beach Residence, usually called JBR, combines a public beach with
dining and entertainment. The neighbourhood suits visitors who want coastal activity
without leaving an urban setting.

The Dubai Tram connects parts of the Marina and JBR and links with the Metro. Walking
between nearby stops can be pleasant in suitable weather, but bridges, road crossings and
heat can make routes longer than they appear. Taxis are practical for the final connection
to hotels or attractions away from a station.

A coherent half-day combines the Marina promenade, JBR and the beach. Boat tours also
depart from the area, but routes, weather policies and boarding locations vary. Check the
official operator and tourism pages for current schedules and accessibility before
booking.
