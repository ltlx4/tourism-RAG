# Dubai After Dark

Dubai evenings range from waterfront walks and late dining to performances and licensed
nightlife venues. Downtown, Dubai Marina, JBR and many hotels contain clusters of evening
options, so choosing a venue near the day's final activity avoids another long cross-city
journey.

Alcohol is available only through licensed venues and is subject to UAE laws and
venue-specific age, identification and conduct rules. Travelers should never drive after
drinking and should arrange a licensed taxi or other appropriate transport. Dress codes
and admission policies differ and need direct confirmation.

For a quieter evening, consider a Creek walk, a marina promenade, a cultural performance
or dinner with a view. Outdoor comfort changes by season. Event schedules, last entry,
transport service and venue policies are volatile and should be verified on the official
venue page.
