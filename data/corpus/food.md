# Eating in Dubai

Dubai's food scene reflects Emirati traditions and the communities that live in the
city. Visitors can find Gulf dishes alongside South Asian, Iranian, Levantine, African,
Filipino and many other cuisines. Old Dubai is especially useful for an informal food
walk, while Downtown, Jumeirah and Dubai Marina offer broad ranges from casual dining to
special-occasion restaurants.

Useful Emirati dishes to look for include machboos, a spiced rice dish; harees, made from
wheat and meat; luqaimat, small sweet dumplings; and Arabic coffee served with dates.
Menus and recipes vary. Asking restaurant staff about portion sizes and spice levels can
help groups order well.

During Ramadan, visitor dining remains available in Dubai, but operating patterns and
the atmosphere may differ. Travelers should act respectfully and verify current venue
hours. For allergies or strict dietary needs, contact the restaurant directly rather
than relying solely on menu labels or a general guide.
