# Beaches and Coastal Dubai

Dubai has public beaches, beach parks and private hotel beach clubs. Jumeirah's coastline
provides views and swimming, while Kite Beach is associated with an active atmosphere,
watersports and a running track. Jumeirah Beach Residence combines a public beachfront
with dining and access to Dubai Marina.

Beach choice depends on the desired pace. Families may value facilities and easy food
options; active visitors may prefer watersports; travelers seeking a quieter or more
serviced experience may consider a beach club. Respect posted safety flags, lifeguard
instructions and local rules. Sun protection and hydration matter throughout the year.

The coast can pair naturally with Dubai Marina, JBR or Madinat Jumeirah, but it is less
efficient to combine with Old Dubai in a short half-day. Public access, facilities,
watersport availability and swimming conditions can change, so consult the official
tourism listing and the destination's own page on the day of a visit.
