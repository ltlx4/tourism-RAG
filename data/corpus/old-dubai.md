# Old Dubai and the Creek

Dubai Creek is the historic waterway around which the trading city developed. The
neighbourhoods on either side offer a compact culture-focused day: Bur Dubai on the
southwestern bank and Deira on the northeastern bank. Traditional wooden abras cross the
Creek and are a useful way to connect the two areas.

In Bur Dubai, the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood is known for restored courtyard
buildings, narrow lanes, wind-tower architecture, galleries and cultural spaces. Nearby
stops include the textile souk and the Creek waterfront. Dress comfortably and
respectfully when visiting cultural sites.

Across the water in Deira, visitors can explore the spice and gold souks. The souks are
working markets, so allow time to browse and compare before buying. A practical route is
Al Fahidi in the morning, lunch in Bur Dubai, an abra crossing, then the Deira souks later
in the day. Confirm museum access, abra routes and current operating details through the
linked official tourism source before setting out.
