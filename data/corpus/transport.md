# Getting Around Dubai

Dubai's main visitor transport options are the Metro, public buses, trams, marine
transport and taxis. The Metro is often the simplest choice for travel along the city's
main corridor and to several high-traffic districts. A rechargeable nol card is used
across multiple public transport modes. Visitors should use the official Roads and
Transport Authority journey planner for current routes, fares and service times.

The Dubai Tram serves parts of Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Beach Residence and nearby areas.
Taxis are widely available and useful for places that are not close to a station, for
groups, or when heat makes a long walk impractical. Travel times by road can increase
during busy periods, so itinerary plans should leave margin between timed bookings.

For Old Dubai, Metro plus an abra crossing creates a practical sightseeing route. For a
trip combining widely separated areas, grouping each day geographically reduces transit
time. Walking is comfortable inside individual districts in suitable weather, but the
city is spread out and map distances can understate heat exposure.
