# Shopping in Dubai

Dubai offers two distinct shopping experiences: large air-conditioned malls and
traditional market districts. Dubai Mall and Mall of the Emirates combine retail with
restaurants and indoor attractions, making them practical during the hottest part of the
day. Their scale means a visit can occupy several hours.

The textile, spice and gold souks around Bur Dubai and Deira are better paired with a
Creek and Old Dubai itinerary. These are active markets where browsing and comparison are
part of the experience. Travelers should understand the item, quality and final price
before purchasing rather than treating bargaining as a fixed formula.

Shopping festivals, tax procedures, delivery terms and store hours change. Verify current
campaigns and visitor refund rules with official sources. Keep receipts for significant
purchases and check customs requirements for the destination country.
